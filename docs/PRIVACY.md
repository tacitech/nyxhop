# Privacy

* The software does not connect to us. There is no telemetry, no activation server, no update check.
* To issue a licence we need the board's DNA (the FPGA chip's factory serial number, not personal
  data) and an email address. We keep the email and the DNA in our licence records to count free
  licences and to reissue a lost file. We do not share them.
* The licence file stored on your board contains the DNA, the licence data and the name you gave
  us, if any. It does not contain your email.
* Paid purchases are handled by our payment provider; we receive the order reference and the
  email, not payment details.
