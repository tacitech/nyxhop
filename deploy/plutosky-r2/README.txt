NyxHop on PlutoSky R2

1. Plug the board's USB port into the PC. Not DEBUG (the middle Type-C port).
2. Run install.bat (Windows) or install.sh (Linux/Mac). Wait for "Done".
   2-3 min. The board reboots twice on its own. Don't unplug.
3. Open the NyxHop app, pick Ground station or Aircraft.
   The board is 192.168.1.10 over Ethernet.
4. Antennas on TX1 and RX1 before you transmit. First test: boards 1-3 m apart.
5. 20 hours free. For a licence, send the DNA (Licence page in the app) to
   contact@tacitek.com. You get a file back. Paste it into the app.

No PlutoSDR drive? Wrong port or not enough power. Plug in both Type-C ports.
The whole thing, with pictures: "Install guide.pdf".
